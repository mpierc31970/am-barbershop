**THESIS.** CDS is a fan deck: paths and add-ons are colour blades on one rivet, each carrying its own name and
its live catalog price, so choosing a website reads like choosing a colour, not configuring software. Refuses the
gradient SaaS hero with three icon cards and a logo strip, and the austere editorial manifesto.

**OWN-WORLD.** White card stock and cool stock grey; saturated chip fields own whole regions (cobalt = Standard,
berry = Studio, leaf = money that stays yours, tangerine = what a platform keeps, marigold/violet/teal/rose/sky =
the add-on ring); ink action buttons; 16px-radius blades with a rivet ring; Anybody across its width axis
(condensed blade labels, wide CDS wordmark) over Hanken Grotesk; tabular price runs; fine print printed loud on a
marigold band; an ink footer as the deck's back cover.

**STORY.** A salon owner on her phone learns in one line what CDS is, sees the price is listed and that nobody
takes a cut of her sales, opens a blade or two, and taps Start your site with her path already chosen.

**FIRST VIEWPORT.** Desktop: left six columns, the H1 at display size, a two-line lead, the pitch line on its own
with a leaf rivet, ink "Start your site" (52px) plus "See pricing"; right six columns, the fanned deck (Standard,
Studio, Shop, then add-ons, live prices) with the Standard blade lifted. Phone: H1 48px, lead, pitch, full-width
CTA inside the first 600px, then the closed deck rows.

**FORM.** The colour fan deck (salon swatch ring / paint fan deck), #4 of 7 on the ordered list; seed key
015984aa.

**FINISH.** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict,
DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
