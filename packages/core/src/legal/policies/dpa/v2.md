## Roles

Your site collects personal data from your customers, such as names, email addresses and form messages. For that data, you decide what is collected and why. A&M Barber Studio processes it on your behalf and only on your instructions, which are the settings you choose in your dashboard.

## What A&M Barber Studio does with the data

A&M Barber Studio stores, shows and sends the data only to run your site and the add-ons you install. A&M Barber Studio does not sell it, does not use it for advertising and does not combine it with other customers' data.

## Security

A&M Barber Studio keeps your data on servers it controls, sends it over encrypted connections, limits who at A&M Barber Studio can see it, and logs every time A&M Barber Studio staff open your dashboard. A&M Barber Studio backs the data up daily.
