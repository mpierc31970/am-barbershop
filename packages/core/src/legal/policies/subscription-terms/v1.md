## How billing works

Your plan is month to month. Each month you pay your path's base fee and the monthly fee of each add-on you have installed. Support is included. The Care Plan is an optional upgrade.

## Paying annually

<!-- clause:annual-two-months-free -->
You can pay annually instead. Annual billing gives you 2 months free on the base fee and on flat monthly add-ons. Metered items, such as email allowances and overages, are never prepaid; they keep their monthly allowance and are billed monthly as used.

## Adding and removing add-ons

Adding an add-on starts its fee from the day you add it. Removing one stops its fee at the end of the current period and keeps its data, so adding it back restores it.

## If a payment lapses

<!-- clause:lapse-disables -->
If a payment fails and is not fixed, CDS switches off the add-ons the payment covers. A lapse disables; it never deletes your data. Paying the balance switches everything back on.

## Cancelling

You can cancel at any time from your dashboard. Your site stays up until the end of the paid period. Annual payments are not refunded for unused months.

## Price changes

CDS tells you at least 30 days before a price change reaches your bill.
