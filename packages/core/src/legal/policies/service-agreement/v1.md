## Who this agreement is between

This agreement is between Connections Design Studios ("CDS", "we") and the business that signs it ("you"). It covers the website CDS builds and hosts for you, the add-ons you choose, and any custom work you order.

## What CDS does

CDS builds the first version of your site from your setup answers, hosts it, keeps it running and gives you a dashboard to edit it. Support is included in every plan.

## Your order

Your path, build fee, add-ons and monthly fees are set by your order, not by this text. Your signed copy includes a schedule called "Your order at signing" so you can see what you ordered. Prices you have not yet ordered can change; CDS will tell you before a change reaches your bill.

## Build fee and payments

The build fee is a fixed price for your path. You can pay it in full, or in up to 6 monthly payments with no surcharge, on the same monthly bill as your plan.

<!-- clause:installments-owed -->
If you cancel your monthly plan before all build payments are made, the remaining build payments are still owed, because your site was delivered.

## If CDS declines your site

<!-- clause:decline-window -->
CDS may decline your site within 5 business days of your payment, before build work starts. If CDS declines, you get a full refund, any remaining build payments are cancelled, and your subscriptions end.

## Payments from your customers

<!-- clause:gateway-ownership -->
You set up and own the payment account your site uses to take payments from your customers, such as your own Stripe account. CDS may help you set it up, but the account is yours.

<!-- clause:merchant-of-record -->
You are the merchant of record for every sale on your site. You pay your payment provider's fees directly, and you handle refunds, disputes and chargebacks with your customers.

<!-- clause:no-payment-processing -->
CDS processes no payments for your customers, never holds your money and takes no cut or platform fee from your sales. CDS bills you only for CDS's own services: your build fee, plan, add-ons and any custom work.

## Custom work

CDS builds custom work only in its one shared platform, never as a separate copy for one business.

<!-- clause:commissioned-ip -->
CDS keeps the rights to everything it builds, including custom blocks and private add-ons made for you. You get to use what you paid for for as long as your plan is active.

<!-- clause:catalog-credit-12mo -->
If CDS turns a feature it built for you into an add-on for every customer, you get 12 months of that add-on free, and its maintenance fee stops.

<!-- clause:maintenance-fees -->
A private add-on carries a monthly maintenance fee, set in its quote, that keeps it working as the platform changes.

<!-- clause:private-addon-eol -->
CDS may retire a private add-on it can no longer maintain. CDS will give you at least 90 days' notice, export its data for you, and stop its maintenance fee when it is retired.

## Your content

You own the words, images and data you put on your site. You give CDS permission to store, copy and show them only so it can run your site. You are responsible for having the rights to what you publish.

## Ending this agreement

You can cancel your plan at any time from your dashboard. Your site stays up until the end of the paid period. Cancelling does not cancel build payments that are still owed.

## Changes to this agreement

CDS may publish a new version of this agreement. You will be asked to accept it the next time you sign in, and you can read what changed before you do.
