## Who this agreement is between

This agreement is between A&M Barber Studio ("we") and the business that signs it ("you"). It covers the website A&M Barber Studio builds and hosts for you, the add-ons you choose, and any custom work you order.

## What A&M Barber Studio does

A&M Barber Studio builds the first version of your site from your setup answers, hosts it, keeps it running and gives you a dashboard to edit it. Support is included in every plan.

## Your order

Your path, build fee, add-ons and monthly fees are set by your order, not by this text. Your signed copy includes a schedule called "Your order at signing" so you can see what you ordered. Prices you have not yet ordered can change; A&M Barber Studio will tell you before a change reaches your bill.

## Build fee and payments

The build fee is a fixed price for your path. You can pay it in full, or in up to 6 monthly payments with no surcharge, on the same monthly bill as your plan.

<!-- clause:installments-owed -->
If you cancel your monthly plan before all build payments are made, the remaining build payments are still owed, because your site was delivered.

## If A&M Barber Studio declines your site

<!-- clause:decline-window -->
A&M Barber Studio may decline your site within 5 business days of your payment, before build work starts. If A&M Barber Studio declines, you get a full refund, any remaining build payments are cancelled, and your subscriptions end.

## Payments from your customers

<!-- clause:gateway-ownership -->
Your shop takes payment from your customers in store only. Your site takes no online payments, deposits or card details. Any payment account you use is yours.

<!-- clause:merchant-of-record -->
You are the merchant of record for every sale to your customers. You pay any payment provider's fees directly, and you handle refunds, disputes and chargebacks with your customers.

<!-- clause:no-payment-processing -->
A&M Barber Studio processes no payments for your customers, never holds your money and takes no cut or platform fee from your sales. A&M Barber Studio bills you only for A&M Barber Studio's own services: your build fee, plan, add-ons and any custom work.

## Custom work

A&M Barber Studio builds custom work only in its one shared platform, never as a separate copy for one business.

<!-- clause:commissioned-ip -->
A&M Barber Studio keeps the rights to everything it builds, including custom blocks and private add-ons made for you. You get to use what you paid for for as long as your plan is active.

<!-- clause:catalog-credit-12mo -->
If A&M Barber Studio turns a feature it built for you into an add-on for every customer, you get 12 months of that add-on free, and its maintenance fee stops.

<!-- clause:maintenance-fees -->
A private add-on carries a monthly maintenance fee, set in its quote, that keeps it working as the platform changes.

<!-- clause:private-addon-eol -->
A&M Barber Studio may retire a private add-on it can no longer maintain. A&M Barber Studio will give you at least 90 days' notice, export its data for you, and stop its maintenance fee when it is retired.

## Your content

You own the words, images and data you put on your site. You give A&M Barber Studio permission to store, copy and show them only so it can run your site. You are responsible for having the rights to what you publish.

## Ending this agreement

You can cancel your plan at any time from your dashboard. Your site stays up until the end of the paid period. Cancelling does not cancel build payments that are still owed.

## Changes to this agreement

A&M Barber Studio may publish a new version of this agreement. You will be asked to accept it the next time you sign in, and you can read what changed before you do.
