## Why this policy exists

CDS hosts every site on one shared platform, so one site can put every other customer at risk. This policy says which sites CDS hosts, which it reviews first, and what happens when a site breaks the rules. The same lists are shown on CDS's website and in setup before you pay.

## What you agree to

- You give honest answers about your business during setup.
- You publish only content you have the rights to use.
- You don't use your site to send spam, collect data you are not allowed to collect, or attack other systems.
- You keep your sign-in details private and tell CDS if you think someone else has used them.

## Taking a site down

<!-- clause:takedown-right -->
CDS can suspend or take down a site that breaks this policy. Illegal content or a legal demand means immediate suspension. For any other breach, CDS tells you what is wrong and gives you 3 days to fix it before suspending the site. Every step is logged, and you are told by email and in your dashboard.

A suspended site keeps its data. If the problem is fixed, CDS can restore it.

## Reporting a site

Anyone can report a site hosted by CDS through the Report link in the site's footer or by emailing [abuse@connectionsdesignstudios.com](mailto:abuse@connectionsdesignstudios.com).
