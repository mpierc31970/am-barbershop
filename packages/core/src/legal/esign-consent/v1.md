## What you agree to

By ticking the box, you agree to sign CDS's agreements electronically, by typing your name, and to receive these agreements, later versions of them and notices about your account electronically, by email and in your dashboard.

## Paper copies

You can download a copy of what you signed right after signing, and from your dashboard at any time. You can also ask for a paper copy, free of charge, by emailing [support@connectionsdesignstudios.com](mailto:support@connectionsdesignstudios.com).

## Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw this consent at any time by emailing [support@connectionsdesignstudios.com](mailto:support@connectionsdesignstudios.com). Withdrawing does not undo anything you have already signed. Because CDS runs your site and your account online, withdrawing means CDS can no longer provide the service, and your plan will be cancelled.

## Keeping your email address current

CDS sends notices to the email address on your account. You can change it in your dashboard settings. Keep it current so you receive every notice.

## What you need

A current web browser that can open and print an HTML file, and an email address you can read.
