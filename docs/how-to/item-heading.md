# How to: Heading item

Owner-hub steps use the P3 routes; re-record when P11 completes the hub shell.

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

A heading inside a column of a Columns block (CONT-03). The same item editor is used for pop-up content.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/sign-in` | Type your **Email** and **Password**, click **Sign in** | The **Website** screen opens at `/hub/website` on the **Pages** tab |
| 2 | `/hub/website?tab=pages` | Click the page title | The page builder opens at `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` |
| 3 | `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` | Click a **Columns** row in the block list (or click **Add block** and pick **Columns**) | The Columns form opens with its columns |
| 4 | Columns form, a column | Click **Add item**, then **Heading** | A new Heading item is added to that column and its form opens |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Heading item form | Type the **Heading** | — |
| 2 | Heading item form, **Heading size** | Choose **Section heading (H2)** or **Sub-heading (H3)** | The chosen card is selected |
| 3 | Heading item form | Click **Save item** | The item row reads `Heading · "<your text>"` and the header reads **Draft saved · Unpublished changes** |
| 4 | Page header | Click **Preview page**, then **Publish changes** | A toast reads **Published. Your changes are live.** |
