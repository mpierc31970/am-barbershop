# How to: Messages with us

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Messages is one ongoing conversation between your site and the Support team, plus the notes Support pins for you. Use it for quick questions and updates; for something broken or a change you want tracked, use [Get help](get-help.md) instead. Everyone on your site with support access sees the same conversation. It is a record: messages can't be edited or deleted.

## Activate

Nothing to turn on: Messages is the first tab of Support for everyone on your site.

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any hub screen | Click **Support** at the bottom of the sidebar | `/hub/support` opens on **Messages**. With no messages yet it reads "No messages yet" and "Write to Support any time.", with the **Write to Support** box below |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Read the top of the tab | Pinned notes from Support come first: each a Support card with the Support mark, the title, "Pinned", the text and "From Support · 9 Oct". An unread note shows **New**; opening the tab marks it read. Older notes sit under **Earlier notes (3)**; click it to open them |
| 2 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Scroll to **Messages with us** | The conversation, oldest at the top, grouped by day ("Today", "Yesterday", "Thu 9 Oct"). Support messages sit on the Support card with the writer's first name and "Support" (e.g. "Mara · Support"); yours read "You". A line "New since your last visit" marks where you left off |
| 3 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Type in **Write to Support** and click **Send message** (or press Ctrl+Enter, Cmd+Enter on a Mac) | Your message appears at the bottom straight away. Under the box: "Support usually replies within 2 business days. For something broken on your site, Get help opens a ticket we track." |
| 4 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Click **Attach files**, pick a file (or paste a screenshot into the box, or drop files on it), then **Send message** | A chip shows "Uploading 40%" while it loads. Up to 5 files, 10 MB each: images, PDF, text, spreadsheets and Office documents. A pasted screenshot is named like "Screenshot 12 Oct, 2:14 pm.png". Click **Remove {name}** to take one off before sending |
| 5 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Look at a file you just sent | It shows **Checking file** until Support's safety check passes; then it can be downloaded. Images show as small thumbnails: click one to preview it, then **Download** |
| 6 | `/hub/support?tab=messages`, left open | Wait for Support to reply | The reply appears within about 20 seconds, and screen readers hear "1 new message from Support". If you have scrolled up, a **1 new message** button appears; click it to jump down |
| 7 | Any hub screen | When Support writes while you are elsewhere | The bell shows "New message from Support" with the small Support mark, Today's **Needs you** shows it with **Read message**, and you get an email (set this in Profile › Alerts, row **Messages from Support**) |
| 8 | `/hub/support?tab=messages`, more than 50 messages | Click **Show earlier messages** at the top | 50 older messages load above, and your place in the conversation stays put |
| 9 | `/hub/today` | When Support pins a note | The **From Support** card shows the note's title and text with **Read the note**, which opens this tab. **Dismiss** hides the card; the note stays here |
