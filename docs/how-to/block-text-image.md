# How to: Text & image block

Owner-hub steps use the P3 routes; re-record when P11 completes the hub shell.

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

A heading and text beside an image, with an optional quote, list, button and phone number (CONT-03).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/sign-in` | Type your **Email** and **Password**, click **Sign in** | The **Website** screen opens at `/hub/website` on the **Pages** tab |
| 2 | `/hub/website?tab=pages` | Click the page title | The page builder opens at `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` |
| 3 | `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` | Click **Add block** | The right pane shows **Add a block** |
| 4 | **Add a block** | Under **Blocks**, click **Text & image** | A Text & image row is added and selected, and the **Text & image block** form opens |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Text & image block** form | Fill **Heading**, then type in **Text**. Use the toolbar for **Bold**, **Italic**, **Underline**, **Bulleted list** or **Numbered list** | The text shows the formatting as you type |
| 2 | **Text & image block** form | Paste an **Image address** and fill **Image description** | Both fields hold the values. A missing description shows an error under the field |
| 3 | **Text & image block** form | Optionally fill **Quote**, **List items** (one item per line), **Button text** + **Button link**, and **Phone** | — |
| 4 | **Background** | Choose **Page color** or **Alternate color** | The chosen card is selected |
| 5 | Form footer | Click **Save block** | **Saved** shows for 3 seconds. The header reads **Draft saved · Unpublished changes** |
| 6 | Page header | Click **Preview page**, check the block, then click **Publish changes** | A toast reads **Published. Your changes are live.** |
