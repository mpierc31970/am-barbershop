# How to: Switch between sites

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

If you belong to more than one site (for example you own one business and are an editor on another), the hub shows one site at a time. The switcher changes which site you are working on. You never see two sites' data together. Being added to another site is covered in [People and roles](people-and-roles.md).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any hub screen | Accept an invite to a second site, then sign in | The switcher appears: on a computer at the left of the top bar (your business name with an up-down arrow, named "Switch site, current: Rivera Bakery"); on a phone the business name in the header is the switcher. With only one site there is no switcher |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any hub screen | Click the business name | A list titled **Your sites** opens: one row per site with your role there ("Owner", "Editor"…), the current site first with a check mark |
| 2 | **Your sites** | Click another site | The row shows "Switching…", then Today opens for that site, its name is in the top bar and a toast reads "You're now in Lopez Spa." You always land on Today, never on the tab you were on |
| 3 | **Your sites** | Press **Esc** | The list closes and nothing changes |
| 4 | Page builder with unsaved edits | Click another site in **Your sites** | The builder asks about your unsaved changes first; the switch happens only after you choose |
| 5 | Any hub screen, after your access to a site is removed | Open the hub | The hub opens your first remaining site; the removed site is no longer in **Your sites** |
| 6 | **Your sites** | Try a switch when the connection drops | A toast reads "We couldn't switch sites just now. Try again in a minute." and you stay on the current site |
