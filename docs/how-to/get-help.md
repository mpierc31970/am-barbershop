# How to: Get help (file a ticket)

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Get help sends Support a ticket: a numbered request Support tracks until it is solved. Use it when something isn't working, when you want Support to change something, for a how-to question or a billing question. Support replies within 2 business days to problems and questions. Owners, Admins and Editors can file tickets; Viewers can't.

## Activate

Nothing to turn on. Get help is on Support, in Jump to, and at the foot of every help article.

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/support` | Click **Get help** | `/hub/support/tickets/new` opens: a **← Support** link, the heading **Get help** and the section **What do you need?** |
| 2 | Any help article in the help drawer (the **?** on any screen) | Click **Ask Support** after "Still stuck?" | The same form opens with **Question** chosen and **About** set to the screen you were on (for example Footer on the Website › Footer tab) |
| 3 | Any hub screen | Click **Jump to…** in the top bar (or press **Ctrl K**, **⌘ K** on a Mac), type "ticket" and choose **Get help** | The same form opens |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Under **What do you need?**, choose a card: **Something isn't working**, **Change request**, **Question**, **Billing** (Owners and Admins only) or **Suggest a feature** | The card is checked. **Suggest a feature** swaps the form for the idea form (see [Suggest a feature](suggest-a-feature.md)) |
| 2 | `/hub/support/tickets/new`, Change request | Read the box under the cards | "Some changes are a paid service" and "Things you can do in your dashboard are yours; we'll show you how. Doing it for you is a paid service." with a **What support includes** link |
| 3 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Under **What is it about?**, pick from **About** | Whole site (the default), one of your pages, an add-on, or a site part (Header, Footer, Menus). "Pick the closest. You can explain below." |
| 4 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Type a short **Subject** (up to 80 characters), e.g. "change my hours" | After a moment, "This might answer it" lists up to 3 help articles. Click one: it opens in the help drawer and your form stays as it is. (Not shown for Billing) |
| 5 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Fill **Tell us more** (up to 5,000 characters) | The hint under it fits the type, e.g. "What did you expect, and what happened instead?" for a problem |
| 6 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Under **Screenshots or files**, click **Attach files**, drop files, or paste a screenshot into Tell us more | Each file shows a chip with its progress. Up to 5 files, 10 MB each |
| 7 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Read the footer, then click **Send to Support** | The footer reads "Support replies within 2 business days." (problems and questions) or "Support replies here and lets you know by email." (changes and billing), and "We include the screen you came from and your browser, to help us look into it." Sending opens the new ticket at `/hub/support/tickets/<number>` with a toast "Ticket #1042 sent. Support replies within 2 business days." |
| 8 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Click **Send to Support** with a field missing | The form stays, the first missing field is focused and says what's needed, e.g. "Choose what you need." or "Add a short subject." |
| 9 | `/hub/support/tickets/new`, with text typed | Click **Discard** or leave the page | A dialog "Leave without sending?" asks first; **Keep writing** has the focus, **Leave** drops the text |
| 10 | `/hub/support/tickets/new` | Click the **?** next to the heading | This how-to opens |
