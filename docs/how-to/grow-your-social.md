# How to: Grow your social

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

**Grow your social** is a free checklist of small things that help people find you (DASH-16). It sits under **My social accounts** on Marketing › Social, is part of every site (no add-on to buy) and needs no setup. Tips are grouped by **Every account** and then one group per social account on the list, and they fit your kind of business (a salon sees "how to book" tips, a shop sees "how to find you" tips). Support never posts for you, never asks for your social passwords and never shows follower counts. Adding, editing and copying bio links is in [My social accounts](social-accounts.md).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hub sidebar | Click **Marketing**, then the **Social** tab | `/hub/marketing?tab=social` shows **My social accounts**, **Add an account** and, below them, the heading **Grow your social** with "Small things that help people find you. Tick them off as you go." and a count such as "0 of 6 done" |
| 2 | **Grow your social** (no accounts yet) | Read the list | Only the **Every account** group shows, with the line "Add an account above to see tips for it." |
| 3 | **Add an account** | Click **Instagram**, type `https://instagram.com/riverabakery`, click **Save account** | An **Instagram** group joins **Grow your social** under **Every account**, and the count's total goes up |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Grow your social** › **Every account** | Tick **Use the same business name and profile photo on every account** | The tick saves straight away (screen readers hear "Saved") and the count goes up, e.g. "1 of 9 done" |
| 2 | **Grow your social** › **Instagram** | Copy the Instagram bio link from **My social accounts** (**Copy link for your Instagram bio**), paste it into your Instagram bio, then tick **Put your bio link from this page in your Instagram bio** | The tip is ticked and the count goes up |
| 3 | Same tip | Untick it | The tick clears, "Saved" is announced and the count goes down |
| 4 | `/hub/marketing?tab=social` (signed in as another team member) | Open the tab | The same ticks show: the checklist is shared by everyone on the site |
| 5 | **My social accounts** › Instagram row | Click **More for Instagram**, then **Remove** | The **Instagram** group leaves **Grow your social**; its ticks are kept and come back if Instagram is added again |
| 6 | `/hub/marketing?tab=social` (signed in as a Viewer) | Open the tab | The checklist and its ticks show, but the boxes can't be changed |

## Good to know

- **If a tick won't save:** the box goes back to how it was and a message under the heading says so. Nothing else on the page is affected; try again.
- **Accounts added with Add another** (for example Etsy) get no group of their own; the **Every account** tips still apply to them.
- **Results** from bio links show the next day in **Reports › Social** ([Read your Reports](reports.md)).
