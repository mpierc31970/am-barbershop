# How to: Content notices and suspension

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Every site CDS hosts follows the same hosting rules. When CDS finds something on your site that breaks them (or the law), it first sends a **notice to fix**: you get 3 days to change it and the site stays online. If it isn't fixed by then, or in a serious case right away, CDS **suspends** the site: visitors see a plain "temporarily unavailable" page, but your hub and CDS Support keep working. When CDS lifts the suspension, the site comes back exactly as it was. Nothing is deleted. Separately, if a visitor reports one of your pages, you get an email saying which page and why, never who reported it.

Everyone on your team sees the notice in the hub. The emails go to the Owner and Admins.

## Activate

Nothing to turn on: notices and reports reach you automatically.

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/profile` | Check that your email address is current | Notice, suspension and report emails reach you (Owner and Admins) |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Your email inbox | Open "Action needed on Rivera Bakery by Thu 16 Oct" | "CDS found something on your site that breaks the hosting rules. Fix it by Thu 16 Oct or your site goes offline.", then what to change, then "If you think this is a mistake, reply in CDS Support." and a **See what to fix** button |
| 2 | Any hub page, for example `/hub/today` | Read the strip under the top bar | The same sentence with the date in bold ("Thu 16 Oct, 5:00 pm") and a **See what to fix** link |
| 3 | Strip | Click **See what to fix** | `/hub/support?tab=messages` opens on CDS's note, which says exactly what to change and where |
| 4 | The page or file named in the note | Change or remove it in the page builder or the media library, then publish | The live site no longer shows it |
| 5 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Reply to CDS's note that it's fixed (or click **Get help** to ask a question) | CDS checks the site and marks it fixed. The strip goes away |
| 6 | Your email inbox, notice not fixed in time (or a serious case) | Open "Rivera Bakery is offline" | "Your site is offline. CDS suspended it on 16 Oct. Your hub and CDS Support still work." with the reason and an **Open CDS Support** button |
| 7 | Any hub page | Read the red strip under the top bar | "Your site is offline. CDS suspended it on 16 Oct. Your hub and CDS Support still work." with a **Read why** link to the CDS note in Messages |
| 8 | Your site's address, in a private window | Open any page | A plain page "This site is temporarily unavailable." and "Please check back later." No branding, no reason, nothing from your site. Search engines are told not to index it |
| 9 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Fix what the note names and reply to CDS | CDS reviews it and lifts the suspension |
| 10 | Your email inbox | Open "Rivera Bakery is back online" | "Your site is back online. Thanks for sorting it out." and **Open your hub**. Every page, file, add-on and setting is exactly as it was before the suspension |
| 11 | Your email inbox, after a visitor reported a page | Open "A page on Rivera Bakery was reported" | "Someone reported a page on Rivera Bakery to CDS. CDS reviews every report, usually within 2 business days, and tells you if anything needs to change." with **Page** (its address) and **Reason** (Copyright, Scam or fraud, Illegal content or Something else). You don't need to do anything unless CDS asks |
