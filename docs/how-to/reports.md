# How to: Read your Reports

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Reports shows how your site is doing: visitors, the pages they open, where they come from, page speed, SEO health, messages from your forms, broken addresses and what people searched on Google. Everyone on your team who can see pages can open Reports, Viewers included. Nothing here can be changed, so it is safe to explore.

Before your site goes live, the traffic sections show "Your first numbers appear a day after launch." SEO health still shows, so you can fix it before launch.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub` | Sign in as any member (Owner, Admin, Editor or Viewer) | The hub opens on your dashboard |
| 2 | Sidebar | Click **Reports** | `/hub/reports` opens with the heading **Reports**, a **?** button, the tabs **Site Report** (underlined) and **Social**, and the period links **Last 7 days · Last 30 days · Last 90 days** with **Last 30 days** selected |

Google Analytics and Search Console need no action from you: CDS connects them. Until then both sections read "Connects when CDS finishes setup."

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reports › Site Report | Read **Summary** | One sentence like "In the last 30 days, **1,284 people** visited your site and **23** sent you a message.", then **Visitors** and **Messages** with words like "Up 12% vs the previous 30 days" |
| 2 | Reports › Site Report | Under **Visitors by day**, click **Show as chart** | The day-by-day table turns into a column chart. Hover a column (or Tab to the chart and press the arrow keys) to see "Fri 3 Oct · 84 visitors". Click **Show as table** to go back |
| 3 | Reports › Site Report | Read **Top pages** and **Where visitors come from** | Up to 10 pages with their share of visitors, and each source (Google, Direct, Instagram, other sites) with visitors and messages. Click **Show all {n}** to see every source |
| 4 | Reports › Site Report | Read **SEO health** | A line like "**82 out of 100** · Good", then up to three **Top fixes**. Click **Fix on About us**: the About us page opens with its SEO & sharing panel open. With nothing to fix it reads "Nothing to fix right now." |
| 5 | Reports › Site Report | Read **Page speed** | Loading, Responsiveness and Visual stability for phones and computers, each with **Good**, **Needs work** or **Poor**. With fewer than about 20 visits it reads "Not enough visits yet to measure speed. We need about 20." |
| 6 | Reports › Site Report | Read **Messages from your forms**, then click **Open Inbox** | The message count for the period and its change; the Inbox opens |
| 7 | Reports › Site Report | Read **Pages not found** | Addresses people opened that don't exist, how often and when last seen. Fix the old link in your menu or buttons. None: "No broken addresses in this period." |
| 8 | Reports › Site Report | Read **What people searched on Google** and **Google Analytics** | Searches with times shown and clicks; Google's visitor count with the note that it only counts visitors who accept cookies. Before CDS connects them: "Connects when CDS finishes setup." |
| 9 | Reports | Click **Last 7 days** | Every section updates to the last 7 days and compares with the 7 days before |
| 10 | Reports | Click the **Social** tab | A sentence like "**Instagram** sent **84 visitors** and **6 messages** in the last 30 days." and a table of Account · Visitors · Messages. Follower counts are never shown. No visits yet: "No visits from your bio links yet." with **Add your bio links** |
