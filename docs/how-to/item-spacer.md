# How to: Spacer item

Owner-hub steps use the P3 routes; re-record when P11 completes the hub shell.

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Empty space between items in a column, in three fixed sizes (CONT-03).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/sign-in` | Type your **Email** and **Password**, click **Sign in** | The **Website** screen opens at `/hub/website` on the **Pages** tab |
| 2 | `/hub/website?tab=pages` | Click the page title | The page builder opens at `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` |
| 3 | `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` | Click a **Columns** row in the block list | The Columns form opens |
| 4 | Columns form, a column | Click **Add item**, then **Spacer** | A new Spacer item is added and its form opens |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Spacer item form, **Space** | Choose **Small** (24px), **Medium** (48px) or **Large** (64px) | The chosen card is selected |
| 2 | Spacer item form | Click **Save item** | The item row reads `Spacer · Medium` (or the size chosen) |
| 3 | Columns form | Drag the spacer between two items, or use **Move up** / **Move down** in its menu | The spacer sits where you dropped it and **Saved** flashes |
| 4 | Page header | Click **Preview page**, then **Publish changes** | A toast reads **Published. Your changes are live.** |
