# How to: Suggested blocks

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Ready-made starting points in the block picker (CONT-27, D183). Each adds normal blocks you then edit like any other. **Contact form and map** adds two blocks at once.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/sign-in` | Type your **Email** and **Password**, click **Sign in** | The **Website** screen opens at `/hub/website` on the **Pages** tab |
| 2 | `/hub/website?tab=pages` | Click the page title | The page builder opens at `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` |
| 3 | `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` | Select the block the new one should follow, then click **Add block** | The right pane shows **Add a block** |
| 4 | **Add a block** | Scroll to **Suggested** | Six tiles, each with a small layout picture: **Photo left, text right**, **Three features**, **Photo gallery**, **Questions and answers**, **Contact form and map**, **Bio with photo** |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Suggested** | Click **Photo left, text right** | A Columns block (40/60) is added with an image on the left and a heading and text on the right, and its form opens |
| 2 | **Suggested** | Click **Three features** | A Columns block (three equal columns) is added, each with a heading and text |
| 3 | **Suggested** | Click **Photo gallery** | A Gallery block headed "Our work" is added in the Mosaic style. Add photos in its form |
| 4 | **Suggested** | Click **Questions and answers** | An FAQ block is added with three starter questions that suit your business, for example "How do I book?". Questions stay hidden on your site until you write their answers |
| 5 | **Suggested** | Click **Contact form and map** | Two blocks are added in order: a Columns block (60/40) with "Get in touch", a short line and a Form item on the left and your address and phone on the right, then a Map block. The first one opens |
| 6 | **Suggested** | Click **Bio with photo** | A Text & image block headed "About me" is added and its form opens. Choose your photo under **Image** and write a few lines about yourself under **Text**. On your site the photo shows on the left and your words on the right (on a phone your words come first, then the photo) |
| 7 | Columns form, **Form** item | Click the Form item and choose a form | Contact form and map adds two blocks; choose the form in the Form item. If your site had no form yet, a new "Contact" form was made for it |
| 8 | Form footer | Click **Save block** on each block you changed | **Saved** shows for 3 seconds. The header reads **Draft saved · Unpublished changes** |
| 9 | Page header | Click **Preview page**, then **Publish changes** | A toast reads **Published. Your changes are live.** |
| 10 | Block list | Drag a block by its grip handle, or click **More actions** (…) on its row and then **Move up** / **Move down** | The block moves to its new place in the list and the order is saved at once; the new position is announced |
