# How to: Alert settings

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

This is the short route to your alert choices. The full walk-through of the profile page (quiet hours, devices, tours, sensitive-practice mode) is [Your profile and alerts](profile-and-alerts.md); it is not repeated here. The monthly site email is covered in [Monthly site email](monthly-site-email.md).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any hub screen | Click the bell, then **Alert settings** at the bottom of the **Alerts** panel | `/hub/profile` opens scrolled to **Alerts**, with "Choose how each alert reaches you. The bell always shows everything." |
| 2 | Any hub screen | Click the profile button (**Your profile and sign out**), then **Your profile and alerts** | The same page opens at the top, heading **Your profile and alerts** |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/profile` › **Alerts** | Read the table | Columns **Alert**, **Bell**, **Email**, **Phone**, grouped **Urgent** ("Sent straight away"), **Normal** ("Sent straight away; phone alerts wait for quiet hours") and **Summary** ("Collected into one email at 7am"). Only alerts your role covers are listed |
| 2 | `/hub/profile` › **Alerts** | Switch **Phone** off on one row | **Saved** shows beside the **Alerts** heading; that alert still shows in the bell but no longer reaches your phone |
| 3 | `/hub/profile` › **Alerts** | Click **Reset to defaults**, then **Undo** | A toast reads "Alerts reset to the defaults." with **Undo**; **Undo** puts your choices back |
| 4 | `/hub/profile` › **Quiet hours** | Set **From** and **Until** | Phone alerts that arrive at night wait until morning and come as one summary, in your business's time zone (details in [Your profile and alerts](profile-and-alerts.md)) |
