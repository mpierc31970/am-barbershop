# How to: Keep your business info up to date

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

**Settings › Business info** holds the details your website shows about your business: its name, tagline, logo, style, phone, email, address, service area, time zone and social links. You gave most of them during setup. Change one here and your site uses it on the next visit. Every field saves by itself: there is no Save button.

Owners and Admins edit. Editors see every field read-only. Viewers have no Settings.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Hub sidebar | Click **Settings** | **Settings** opens on the **Business info** tab, with the groups **Your business**, **How customers reach you**, **Where you are** and **Social links**, then **Your data** |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/settings?tab=business` › **Your business** | Change **Business name** and click outside the field | Under the **Settings** heading "Saving…" shows, then "Saved". The top bar shows the new name after the next page load |
| 2 | **Your business** | Change **Tagline**, then click outside the field | "Saved" shows; the tagline updates on your site |
| 3 | **Your business** › **Logo** | Click the logo field and pick an image from your media, or upload one | The logo shows in the field and "Saved" shows; your site header uses it |
| 4 | **Your business** › **Your site's style** | Click another font pairing | The pairing is selected and "Saved" shows; your site's headings and text use it |
| 5 | **Your business** | Turn **Keep client names out of phone and email alerts** on or off | "Saved" shows. When on, push and email alerts use safe titles with no client name. It is on for therapy, counseling and health businesses unless you turn it off |
| 6 | **How customers reach you** | Change **Phone** or **Public email**, then click outside the field | "Saved" shows; the footer and contact details on your site use the new value |
| 7 | **Where you are** | Change **Street address**, **City**, **State**, **ZIP** or **Service area** | "Saved" shows after each field |
| 8 | **Where you are** › **Time zone** | Pick your time zone | "Saved" shows. Today, alerts, quiet hours and reports now use this time zone |
| 9 | **Social links** | Click **Add a social link**, choose a **Platform** and paste the full address starting with `https://` | "Saved" shows and the icon appears in your site footer. The same list, with bio links, is on **Marketing › Social** |
| 10 | **How customers reach you** | Type an address that isn't a valid email in **Public email** and click outside | The field says what to fix and "Not saved" shows; nothing changes on your site |
| 11 | As an Editor | Open **Settings** | Every field is read-only under the line "Only the owner or an admin can change settings." |
