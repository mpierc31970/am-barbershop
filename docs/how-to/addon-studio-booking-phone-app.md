# How to: Set up the app on your phone

Put the hub on your phone like an app and get a buzz when a client books with you. Do it once per phone. On iPhone you need iOS 16.4 or later for alerts.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | iPhone: Safari | Open **Safari** (it must be Safari) and go to `https://barber.needtobeedgy.com/sign-in` | The A&M sign-in page opens |
| 2 | iPhone: Safari | Sign in | Your hub opens |
| 3 | iPhone: Safari | Tap **Share** (the square with the arrow), then **Add to Home Screen** | The add screen opens |
| 4 | iPhone: Add to Home Screen | Keep **Open as Web App** on and tap **Add** | The A&M icon is on your Home Screen |
| 5 | Android: Chrome | Open **Chrome** and go to `https://barber.needtobeedgy.com/sign-in` | The A&M sign-in page opens |
| 6 | Android: Chrome | Sign in | Your hub opens |
| 7 | Android: Chrome | Tap the **⋮** menu, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**), then **Install** | The A&M icon is on your Home screen |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Phone Home Screen | Open the hub from the **A&M** icon (on iPhone, alerts only work from the icon) and sign in again if asked | The hub opens full screen, with no browser bar |
| 2 | `/hub/today` › **Get alerts on your phone** | Tap **Turn on alerts** | The phone asks to allow notifications |
| 3 | Notification prompt | Tap **Allow** | "Alerts are on for this device." shows |
| 4 | Card not on Today | Tap your profile button (top right), then **Alerts**, then **Alert settings**. Under **Phones and computers** tap **Turn on alerts on this device**, then **Allow** | "Alerts are on for this device." shows and your phone is listed |
| 5 | No buzz on iPhone | Open **Settings** › **Notifications** › **A&M Barber Studio** and turn **Allow Notifications** on | Alerts arrive |
| 6 | No buzz on Android | Open **Settings** › **Apps** › **Chrome** (or the A&M app) › **Notifications** and turn them on | Alerts arrive |
| 7 | Good to know | Each phone turns alerts on once. You only get alerts for your own bookings. Removing the icon turns alerts off | Nothing else to set |

## Change your password

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any hub screen | Tap your profile button (top right), then **Alerts**, then **Alert settings** (or go to `/hub/profile`) | Your profile opens |
| 2 | `/hub/profile` › **Change your password** | Enter your current password, then your new password twice (10 or more characters) and tap **Change password** | "Your password is changed." shows and you stay signed in |
| 3 | Sign-in email | To change the email you sign in with, ask the shop owner | The owner updates it for you |
