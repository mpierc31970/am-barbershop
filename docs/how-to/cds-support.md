# How to: Support

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Support is how you reach us: questions, changes, or something not working. Support is included in every plan (see [What support includes](what-support-includes.md)). It is the one screen in your hub that carries the Support name and mark, so you always know when Support is speaking.

It has four tabs: **Messages** (your ongoing conversation and notes from Support), **Tickets** (each request Support tracks), **Documents** (files Support shares and files in your tickets) and **Ideas** (features suggested by Support sites). Owners, Admins and Editors can write; Viewers can read everything but not write.

## Activate

Nothing to turn on: **Support** is pinned at the bottom of the sidebar for everyone on your site.

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Any hub screen | Click **Support** at the bottom of the sidebar (on a phone: open the menu first) | `/hub/support` opens on **Messages**: the Support mark, the heading **Support**, the line "From the Connections Design Studios team · What support includes", the button **Get help**, and the tabs **Messages · Tickets · Documents · Ideas**. A tab with something new shows a count, e.g. "Messages · 2" |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/support` | Click **Get help** (top right; on a phone it is the full-width button under the byline) | The Get help form opens at `/hub/support/tickets/new` (see [Get help](get-help.md)) |
| 2 | `/hub/support?tab=messages` | Write in **Write to Support** and click **Send message** | Your message is added to **Messages with us** (see [Messages with us](messages-with-cds.md)) |
| 3 | `/hub/support` | Click **Tickets** | **Your tickets** lists every ticket, Waiting on you first. Click one to follow it (see [Follow a ticket](follow-a-ticket.md)) |
| 4 | `/hub/support?tab=tickets` (Owner) | Read **Your support** under the list | "Support · Included in your plan" with **What support includes**, and the Care Plan row: "On · 25 of 60 minutes used in October" with **Settings › Billing**, or "Not on your plan" with **About the Care Plan** |
| 5 | `/hub/support` | Click **Documents** | **Shared by Support** and **From your tickets and messages** list your files (see [Documents from Support](documents-from-cds.md)) |
| 6 | `/hub/support` | Click **Ideas** | Feature ideas from Support sites, with votes (see [Suggest a feature](suggest-a-feature.md)) |
| 7 | `/hub/support` | Click **What support includes** in the byline | The [What support includes](what-support-includes.md) article opens |
| 8 | `/hub/support` (Viewer) | Open any tab | Everything reads normally, but there is no **Get help** and no composer; the line reads "You can read support but not write. Ask your site owner for Editor access." |
| 9 | `/hub/support`, any tab | Read the footer | "Can't use your hub right now? Email {address}." Click the address to open your email app with the subject "Help with {your business}" and your site's name and id at the bottom |
| 10 | `/hub/support`, any tab | Click the **?** next to the heading | The how-to for the tab you are on opens (Messages, Tickets, Documents or Ideas) |
