# How to: Review your site before launch

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Before Support launches your site, you look over every page on its preview address, confirm your key details, check it on your phone and send a test message. When all six items are done, you click **Ready to launch** and Support gives it a final look.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Today (`/hub`), signed in as the **Owner**, once Support has your site ready for review | Click **Continue review** on the launch review card | **Review your site** (`/hub/review`) opens with "0 of 6 done" and the six items |
| 2 | The same screen, signed in as an **Admin** | Read the list | The items show without buttons, and the line reads "Only the owner can approve the launch." |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Every page looked over** | Click **Review on your preview address** | Your site opens in a new tab on its preview address with the strip "Review mode · Page 1 of 7 · Home" and **Back to your review**; a bar at the bottom shows **Looks good** · **Needs a change** · **Previous page** · **Next page** |
| 2 | The review bar | Look over the page, then click **Looks good** | The next unreviewed page opens and the strip reads "Marked Home as looks good." After the last page: "All pages looked over." |
| 3 | The review bar, a page that needs work | Click **Needs a change**, write **Your note** (at least 10 characters, like "Change the phone number in the footer."), click **Send to Support** | You return to the page and the strip reads "Sent. Support will make the change and tell you." **Changes you asked for** counts it |
| 4 | **Your key details confirmed** | Click **Confirm your details**, check business name, phone, email, address and hours (**Edit** fixes any), then click **Confirm these details** | A toast reads "Key details confirmed." If your email isn't confirmed yet: "Confirm your email address first. We sent the link to maria@gmail.com." |
| 5 | **Checked on your phone** | Click **Check on your phone** and scan the code with your phone's camera (or **Copy link** and open it on the phone; it works for 60 minutes) | The site opens in review mode on the phone. Tap **Checked on my phone** on the first page; the strip reads "Thanks. Phone check done." (On a computer you can click **I checked it on my phone**.) |
| 6 | **Test form received** | Click **Send a test message**, fill in the contact form on the preview and send it | The site's own thank-you message shows and the strip adds "Test message sent. It's in your Inbox." The item reads "It's in your Inbox" with **Open Inbox**, where the message carries a **Test** badge |
| 7 | **No open change requests** | Wait for Support to finish any changes (**See your requests** shows them) | The item reads "No open change requests" |
| 8 | **Your site goes live at riverabakery.com.** panel | Click **Ready to launch** | The button shows **Sending…**, then "Support is giving it a final look." with "Usually within 1 business day. We'll email you the moment you're live. You can still edit, but changed pages need another look." |
| 9 | The same panel, if you edit a page after sending | Look at the changed page again and mark it **Looks good** | The warning "You changed About us after sending. Look at it again so Support can launch." clears |

If **Ready to launch** is locked, the reasons show under it: "Finish 2 more items above." or "Your domain isn't ready yet. Finish connecting it in Settings › Domain." (see **Connect your domain**).
