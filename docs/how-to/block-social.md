# How to: Social and sharing block

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Your social links as icons, plus share buttons so visitors can pass the page on (CONT-27, D198). The links come live from Settings › Business info. Share buttons are plain links: no tracking scripts load on your site.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/sign-in` | Type your **Email** and **Password**, click **Sign in** | The **Website** screen opens at `/hub/website` on the **Pages** tab |
| 2 | `/hub/website?tab=pages` | Click the page title | The page builder opens at `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` |
| 3 | `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` | Click **Add block** | The right pane shows **Add a block** |
| 4 | **Add a block** | Under **Information**, click **Social and sharing** | A Social and sharing row is added after the selected block and selected, and its form opens |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Social and sharing** form | Type an optional **Heading**, for example "Follow us" | The heading shows your text. Blank shows no heading |
| 2 | **Social and sharing** form | Leave **Show social links** on and read the line under it | "They come from Settings › Business info. Edit social links" and "Showing Instagram and Facebook" (your connected platforms) |
| 3 | **Social and sharing** form | Click **Edit social links** | `/hub/settings?tab=business` opens. Add or change your links, click **Save**, then go back to the page builder |
| 4 | **Social and sharing** form | Leave **Show share buttons** on | Six checkboxes appear: **Facebook**, **X**, **LinkedIn**, **Pinterest**, **Email**, **Copy link**. Facebook, X, Email and Copy link start ticked |
| 5 | **Share buttons** | Tick or untick a network, for example **LinkedIn** | The tick changes. The buttons always show in this order |
| 6 | **Social and sharing** form | Turn both switches off with no social links saved | A grey note reads "Add social links in Settings › Business info, or turn on share buttons." Nothing would show on your site |
| 7 | **Background** | Choose **Page color** or **Alternate color** | The chosen card is selected |
| 8 | Form footer | Click **Save block** | **Saved** shows for 3 seconds. The header reads **Draft saved · Unpublished changes** |
| 9 | Page header | Click **Preview page**, then **Publish changes** | A toast reads **Published. Your changes are live.** |
| 10 | The live page | Scroll to the block | Your social icons in your site's text colour, turning to your main colour on hover. Each opens your profile in a new tab |
| 11 | The live page | Under "Share this page", click **Facebook** | Facebook's share page opens in a new tab with this page's address filled in |
| 12 | The live page | Click **Copy link** | "Link copied" shows for 2 seconds. The page address is on the clipboard |
| 13 | The live page, on a phone | Click **Share** (the first button, phones only) | The phone's share sheet opens |
