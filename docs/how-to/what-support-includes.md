# What support includes

Support is included in every CDS plan: this is what CDS does for you, what counts as a paid service, how fast CDS replies and how to reach the team.

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

This one file is the single source for "What support includes" (SUPPORT-01, D299): the hub Help article at `/hub/help/what-support-includes` shows the step tables below, and the public hub page (P15) reuses the sections that follow. Keep both in step when you edit.

## Included in every plan

1. **Platform upkeep.** CDS keeps your site and dashboard running, updated and secure. Anything CDS built that breaks is fixed free.
2. **How-to help.** Ask by ticket how to do something, and CDS shows you. Every feature also has a how-to in the Help center and training in your dashboard.
3. **Technical setup you can't do in the dashboard.** Your domain and DNS, where your email goes, linking your own payment gateway, verifying your sending address, and your Google Business Profile.
4. **Recovery.** If something goes badly wrong, CDS helps you get back, including a restore from backup after a serious mistake.
5. **Account and billing.** Questions about your plan, your bill, your invoices and your card.
6. **Safety.** Content holds, DMCA (copyright) notices and security incidents.

## What's a paid service

Things you can do in your dashboard are yours; we'll show you how. Doing it for you is a paid service.

Paid services are:

- Copywriting
- Design changes
- Page building
- Product entry
- Newsletters
- Photos
- Logos
- SEO
- Custom work

CDS always sends a price first, and nothing starts until you accept it (see [Accept a quote from CDS](accept-a-quote.md) for how quotes and rates work). With the [Care Plan](care-plan.md), small jobs like these can use your monthly Care hour instead.

## How fast we reply

CDS replies within 2 business days. Business days are Monday to Friday, Central Time.

The promise covers the first reply to a **Something isn't working** or **Question** ticket. Change requests and billing tickets are answered in the ticket too, and you get an email when CDS replies.

## How to reach CDS

- **Get help** on CDS Support opens a ticket CDS tracks (see [Get help](get-help.md)).
- **Messages** on CDS Support is one ongoing conversation with the CDS team (see [Messages with CDS](messages-with-cds.md)).
- Can't use your hub? Email the CDS support address shown at the foot of every CDS Support tab.
