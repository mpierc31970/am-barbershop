# How to: Monthly site email (Your site this month)

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Once your site is live, you get one short email on the 1st of each month: **"Your site this month: {October}"** (DASH-12, D189). It gives five lines (visitors compared with the month before, your top page, your top source, messages from your forms, and a speed or SEO change) and one tip, with a button to your full Site Report. It comes at most once a month, in your business's own time zone.

There is nothing to switch on. It starts after your first full month live: a site that goes live on 20 October gets its first email on 1 December, about November. Everyone on your team who can open Reports gets it (Owner, Admin, Editor, Viewer), and each person can turn it off for themselves.

Support staff: running and scheduling the job is in [Run the monthly site email job](monthly-site-email-job.md).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/today` | Open Today after Support launches your site | The status line reads "Your site is live at {your address}". Before launch no monthly email is sent |
| 2 | Your email inbox, on the 1st after your first full month live | Look for the email from your business name | The subject reads "Your site this month: November" (the month just ended) |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The email | Read the top | The preheader reads like "1,284 visitors and 23 messages." |
| 2 | The email | Read the five lines | Lines such as "Visitors: 1,284, up 12% on October.", "Top page: Menu (412 visits).", your top source, your messages, and one speed or SEO change. Then "One tip: …" with one thing to try this month |
| 3 | The email | Click **See your Site Report** | The hub opens on `/hub/reports` (sign in first if asked), with the same numbers and more detail. See [Read your Reports](reports.md) |
| 4 | `/hub/profile#alerts` | Click your initials in the top bar (**Your profile and sign out**), then **Your profile and alerts**, and scroll to **Alerts** | The **Summary** group lists **Your site this month**, "A monthly email with your visits and results", with **Email** switched on |
| 5 | Your profile › Alerts | Switch **Email** off for **Your site this month** | "Saving…" then **Saved** shows next to the **Alerts** heading. You get no monthly email from next month. Others on your team still get theirs |
| 6 | Your profile › Alerts | Switch **Email** back on | **Saved** shows again, and the next 1st brings the email |
