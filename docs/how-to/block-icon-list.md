# How to: Icon list block

Owner-hub steps use the P7 page builder routes; re-record when P11 completes the hub shell.

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Short points, each with an icon, such as what is included or why people choose you (CONT-27).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/sign-in` | Type your **Email** and **Password**, click **Sign in** | The **Website** screen opens at `/hub/website` on the **Pages** tab |
| 2 | `/hub/website?tab=pages` | Click the page title | The page builder opens at `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` |
| 3 | `/hub/website/pages/[pageId]` | Click **Add block** | The right pane shows **Add a block** |
| 4 | **Add a block** | Under **Information**, click **Icon list** | An Icon list row is added and selected, and the **Icon list block** form opens with 3 item cards (**Item 1** to **Item 3**), each with a check icon |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Icon list block** form | If you like, fill **Heading** | The heading shows your text |
| 2 | **Item 1** card | Click the icon button under **Icon** (it reads "Icon: Check. Change icon") | A panel opens below it with **Search icons** and a grid of 60 icons; the current icon has a ring |
| 3 | Icon panel | Type in **Search icons**, for example "leaf" | Only matching icons stay. No match shows "No icons match. Try another word." |
| 4 | Icon panel | Click an icon. With a keyboard: press the down arrow from the search field, use the arrow keys, Home and End, then press Enter | The panel closes, focus returns to the icon button and the item shows the chosen icon. Escape closes the panel without changing anything |
| 5 | **Item 1** card | Fill **Title**, for example "Free parking", and **Text** if you like (the counter shows up to 200 characters) | The item shows your words |
| 6 | Under the items | Click **Add item** | A new card with a check icon is added at the end and its **Title** is ready to type in. At 12 items the button is greyed out with "Keep this to 12 items or fewer." |
| 7 | An item card | Drag the grip handle, or click **Move Item 2 up** / **Move Item 2 down** | The items change order |
| 8 | An item card | Click **Delete** | The card is removed and a toast reads "Item removed. Undo"; click **Undo** within 8 seconds to bring it back |
| 9 | **Background** | Choose **Page color** or **Alternate color** | The chosen card is selected |
| 10 | Form footer | Click **Save block** | **Saved** shows for 3 seconds. The header reads **Draft saved · Unpublished changes** |
| 11 | Page header | Click **Preview page**, then **Publish changes** | A toast reads **Published. Your changes are live.** |
| 12 | The live page | Scroll to the list | Each item shows its icon in your theme's main color beside its title and text, in columns that wrap on phones. Items without a title are not shown |
