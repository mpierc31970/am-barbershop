# How to: Today

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Today is the first screen after sign-in. One glance answers "where is my site, and is there anything only I can do?" What you see depends on your role: owners see everything, admins see all but billing, editors see content items, and viewers see the numbers only. Alerts and the bell are in [Needs you and the bell](needs-you-and-the-bell.md); turning on phone alerts is in [Alerts on your phone](alerts-on-your-phone.md).

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sign-in, then `/hub` | Sign in to the hub once your site is past setup | `/hub/today` opens. The heading greets you by the time of day in your business's time zone: "Good morning", "Good afternoon" or "Good evening" |
| 2 | Any hub screen | Click **Today** at the top of the sidebar | `/hub/today` opens again |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/today`, while Support builds your site | Read the line under the greeting | A **Being built** badge and "Support is building your site. You can edit anything while we work." with **View preview**, which opens your preview site in a new tab |
| 2 | `/hub/today` › **Your build** | Read the tracker | Five steps: **Received**, **Site assembled**, **Support polishing**, **Your review**, **Live**, with dates on the done steps and the current step highlighted. Under it, **What Support is doing now:** and "Updated 9 Oct", then "You can edit any page while we work. If Support is working on a page, it says so on that page." |
| 3 | `/hub/today`, when your site is ready for review | Read the panel | The badge reads **Ready for your review** and the sentence "Your site is ready. Look it over and tell us what to change before it goes live." The panel **Review your site before it goes live** shows "0 of 6 done" and six checks. Until the review screen ships it reads "Support will email you when your review is ready to start." |
| 4 | `/hub/today`, first visit after launch | Read the page, then click **Show my Today** | "You're live at {your address}" and "Your site is on its own address now. Here are three things worth doing this week.", with **Share your site** (**Add your bio links**), **Check your Google Business Profile** (**How to check it**) and **See your Site Report** (**Open Reports**). **Show my Today** (or any step's link) hides this for good and shows the normal Today |
| 5 | `/hub/today` › **Needs you** | Click the button on a row, for example **Reply** | The screen that finishes it opens. Urgent rows come first with a warning icon. With nothing to do the section reads "You're all caught up." More than 8 rows: click **Show all 12** to see the rest |
| 6 | `/hub/today` › **Your day** | Read the **Inquiries** card, then click **Open Inbox** | Up to three of this week's newest messages ("Dana Lee · Contact form · Tue") and "7 messages this week"; with none it reads "No messages this week." **Open Inbox** opens the Inbox |
| 7 | `/hub/today` › **Your day** | Click **Customize** | A drawer titled **Customize Today** opens: "Choose which cards show and in what order. Only you see this." |
| 8 | **Customize Today** | Switch **Show** off on **Inquiries**, then click **Done** | The change saves at once and the card leaves Today. With every card hidden Today reads "You've hidden all your cards." with **Customize** |
| 9 | **Customize Today** | Use **Move up** / **Move down** (or drag the handle) to reorder, or click **Reset to default order** | The cards on Today follow the new order behind the drawer |
| 10 | `/hub/today` › **This week** | Read the panel, then click **See your Site Report** | **Visitors** and **Messages** for this Monday-to-Sunday week with "12 more than last week", "3 fewer than last week" or "Same as last week". **See your Site Report** opens `/hub/reports` (see [Reports](reports.md)). Before launch it reads "Your first numbers appear a day after launch." |
| 11 | `/hub/today` › **Work in progress** (after you order paid work or an add-on Support sets up) | Read the card | One row per job with **Ordered**, **Support is setting it up**, **Ready** and "Started 9 Oct"; it disappears when the job is done |
| 12 | `/hub/today` › **From Support** | Read the card, then click **Dismiss this card** (the X) | A news item, a tip, or **Getting started with {add-on}** with **Show me how**. Dismissing it is saved for you; the next card appears on your next visit |
| 13 | `/hub/today`, a section that fails to load | Read the section | "We couldn't load this part. The rest of the page is fine." with **Try again**; every other section still shows |
