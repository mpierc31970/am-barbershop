# How to: Check your Google Business Profile

These steps are the recording script for the Remotion + Venice AI how-to videos (D110), so write every step precisely enough to record from.

Your Google Business Profile is the box people see on Google Search and Google Maps when they look up your business. Once your site is live, check that the profile's hours, phone and website match your site, so people who find you on Google reach the right place. Google runs the profile, not CDS: you sign in to Google with your own account, and CDS never asks for your Google password.

## Activate

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/today`, first visit after launch | Under **Check your Google Business Profile**, click **How to check it** | This article opens in the Help center: `/hub/help/google-business-profile` |
| 2 | Any hub screen | Open **Help** (pinned at the bottom of the sidebar), type "Google" in **Search help** and click **Check your Google Business Profile** | The same article opens |

## Use

| # | Screen / route | Action | Expected result |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `/hub/settings?tab=business` and `/hub/settings?tab=team-hours` | Note your business name, address, phone and opening hours | You have the details your site shows, to compare against Google |
| 2 | A new browser tab, `https://www.google.com/business/` | Sign in with the Google account that manages your business | Google shows your business profile, or offers to add your business when you have none yet |
| 3 | Google Business Profile | Compare the name, address, phone and hours with your notes; edit any that differ and save | Google shows the edits as pending until it reviews them (usually within a few days) |
| 4 | Google Business Profile › **Website** | Enter your site's address exactly as it appears in your browser, starting `https://`, and save | Searching for your business on Google shows a **Website** button that opens your site |
| 5 | Google Search | Search for your business name and town | Your profile shows the same hours, phone and website as your site. If Google asks you to verify the business (by post, phone or video), follow its steps; CDS cannot do this for you |
